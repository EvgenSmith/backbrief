Why a meeting notetaker isn't enough

It summarizes blind

The model doesn't know who is on the call, what those people are working on right now, or what the team agreed a month ago. So "Speaker 2 will handle it" is the best it can do.

Action items die on the way to the tracker

Someone has to retype them, check whether the issue already exists, and find the owner. Usually nobody does, and the task quietly disappears.

The corpus isn't yours

Months of what your team said live in a vendor's cloud, on their terms. Your own agents can't read it, and you can't take it with you.

How it works

No bot joins your calls. {{ product }} works from the recording your meeting platform already makes.

  1. Record

    Turn on Zoom cloud recording, as you may already do. When the recording completes, Zoom notifies {{ product }}, and a short post with the title, duration and recording link appears in your team channel within about a minute.

  2. Transcribe

    When the transcript is ready, usually 15 to 60 minutes later, the speech is cleaned up with your team's glossary, so product names and people's names come out spelled the way you spell them.

  3. Understand

    Claude reads the transcript together with your team's context: who is who and what they own, your company profile, and the most recent calls on the same topic. Decisions, agreements and next steps come out separately, each anchored to the minute it was said.

  4. Deliver

    The digest lands in the same Slack thread. Proposed tasks are first checked against your Linear backlog, then posted with Approve and Skip buttons. The call is committed to your vault as one markdown file.

Works today with Zoom, Slack, Linear, GitHub and Claude, orchestrated by your own n8n. Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, our own speech-to-text and more trackers are on the roadmap.

What makes it different

Context, not summaries

Every call is read together with team profiles and recent related calls, so digest number fifty is sharper than digest number one.

Deduplicated against the live backlog

Before a task is proposed, it is matched against open issues. A restated task becomes a comment on the existing issue; an unsure match is flagged for a human.

Every timestamp is real

Each decision, agreement and task links back to the minute it was said. If the timing isn't known, there is no timestamp at all, never a made-up one.

Your data lives in your repo

The vault is plain markdown in a git repository you own: history, diffs, backup and access control included. Delete {{ product }} tomorrow and everything it wrote stays.

A human approves every task

Nothing reaches your tracker without a click. Every Approve and Skip is logged as your team's own training signal, so autonomy can be earned later rather than assumed.

Open at the core

The plugin, templates and pipeline are on GitHub. The full functional spec fits in one file you can hand to any engineer, or any AI, to rebuild it on another stack.

Privacy and security

  • Self-hosted means zero {{ product }} servers. Call content flows only between systems you configure: your meeting platform, your n8n, your model provider key, your git repository, your Slack and your tracker.
  • No bot joins your calls. {{ product }} processes recordings your platform already made. Recording consent stays with you.
  • Telemetry is opt-in and counters only. The receiving API has no field that can hold text, and its source is public.

Questions

Does {{ product }} record our calls?

No. It works from recordings and transcripts your meeting platform already produces, today Zoom cloud recordings. You can also hand it transcripts exported from other tools.

Which languages does it support?

English and Russian are first-class. Digests and tasks are written in the language of the call; file names and metadata stay in English so the vault stays searchable.

Do we need Slack and Linear?

No. Each part can be switched off. Without a tracker, tasks become files in the vault; without Slack, digests live only in the vault.

Where does our data live?

In your own git repository and your own tracker. Self-hosted, nothing passes through us. In the hosted beta, we process calls only to produce those outputs, under a data processing agreement.

What does the open-source version cost?

Nothing. You pay for your own n8n hosting and your model provider's usage.
