How it works

No bot joins your calls. {{ product }} works from the recording your call platform already makes.

  1. Record

    Record the call to your meeting platform's cloud (Zoom today). About a minute after it ends, a short post with the title and recording link appears in your team channel.

  2. Transcribe

    When the transcript is ready, usually within an hour, product and people names are corrected with your team's glossary.

  3. Understand

    Claude reads the transcript with your team's profiles and recent related calls, and pulls out decisions, agreements and next steps, each with its minute.

  4. Deliver

    The digest lands in the call's chat thread, task proposals follow with Approve and Skip, and the call is saved to your git repository.

Questions

Can we keep some calls out?

Yes. {{ product }} only processes calls recorded to the cloud, so record 1:1s, board or HR calls locally or not at all. Automatic private handling for such calls is on the roadmap.

Who approves the tasks?

Someone on your team, in the call's chat thread. Proposals the backlog check is sure about can be approved together in one click; flagged ones wait to be reviewed one by one. Skipped proposals are logged, so you can see what your team doesn't want filed.

Do you support Google Meet, Jira or single sign-on?

Not yet. Today {{ product }} connects to Zoom, Slack, Linear and GitHub, and those are next. Tell us your stack when you book a demo.

Where do people who don't use git read it?

In your team chat, and in the {{ product }} workspace in the browser: calls, decisions, open action items and people. Git is where the record is kept, so it stays yours.

Which languages does it support?

English and Russian, including calls that switch between the two. Digests and tasks are written in the language of the call; file names and metadata stay in English so the record stays searchable.

We already use Granola or Fireflies. Can we keep it?

Yes. Exported transcripts can be filed the same way as cloud recordings. Automatic capture is connected to Zoom cloud recordings today.

What happens to our data if {{ product }} goes away?

Nothing is lost. Everything it wrote is already in your chat, your tracker and your git repository as plain files, and the recordings and transcripts we hold are deleted as the data processing agreement sets out.
