Signing in to a beta workspace

To sign in, you type your work email and we email you a one-time link through our email delivery provider. We keep the link's token, in hashed form, for 15 minutes. After sign-in, a session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. Signing out deletes it. Your workspace's calls are read from your own vault repository with a read-only key you give us, and are shown only to members of your workspace.

The hosted beta

In the hosted beta we process your call recordings and transcripts on your behalf, only to produce the outputs you asked for: digests, task proposals and vault files. For this processing you are the controller and we are the processor. Before any call data is processed, we sign a data processing agreement with you that lists our sub-processors (for example hosting, speech-to-text and language-model providers), where data is processed and how long it is kept. You remain responsible for obtaining any consent that recording your calls requires.
