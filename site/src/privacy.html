2. Two roles: controller and processor

If you took part in a call processed by one of our customers, please contact that customer first; we will help them answer your request.

4. Cookies

The website uses no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The only cookie is a strictly necessary session cookie (bb_session), set when you log in to a workspace and needed to keep you logged in. It lasts up to 14 days and is deleted when you log out. Continuing with Google also sets a short-lived cookie (bb_oauth, 10 minutes) that protects the sign-in from forgery. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent under article 22.2 of the LSSI.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf under written contracts, and only as far as they need to provide their service:

  • Hosting and website functions: Cloudflare, which hosts the website and runs the functions behind the demo form and sign-in.
  • Email delivery: Resend, which sends password-reset links.
  • Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google confirms your email address to us under its own privacy policy.
  • Scheduling: when the demo form shows a booking calendar, the scheduling provider that runs it receives the name and email you entered and the time you pick.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: where required by law, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

The providers used to deliver the {{ product }} service to customers, such as speech-to-text and language-model providers, are listed in the DPA. Calls are processed with Claude through the Anthropic API under commercial terms that do not allow training on customer data.

6. Customer data in the {{ product }} service

  • We process call recordings and transcripts only to produce the outputs the customer asked for, and deliver those outputs to the customer's own tools: its chat, its task tracker and its git repository.
  • We do not use customer data to train models, and we do not share it with other customers.
  • The browser workspace reads the customer's repository with a read-only key and shows its content only to signed-in members of that customer's workspace.
  • Processing region, sub-processors, retention and deletion of customer data are set out in the DPA.
  • The customer is responsible for having a lawful basis to record its calls and for informing call participants, including any consent that applies in their jurisdictions.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, salted password hashes, hashed single-use reset links, signed and HttpOnly session cookies, rate limits on sign-in and forms, least-privilege and read-only access keys, and access to production systems limited to the people who need it. No system is completely secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.
