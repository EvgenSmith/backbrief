# Company profile

## What we do
Acme makes booking and reminder software for small clinics. A fictional
company used to demonstrate Backbrief.

## Products & terminology
- **Acme Book**: the online booking widget clinics embed on their site.
- **Acme Remind**: SMS and email appointment reminders.
- **Self-serve tier**: the $19 plan clinics can buy without a sales call.

## Market & customers
Independent clinics (1–5 practitioners) and small chains up to 30 sites.

## Current priorities
- 2026-10: launch the self-serve tier before the Q4 campaign.
- 2026-10: cut onboarding drop-off between signup and first booking.

## Notes for the summarizer
"DOB" on calls means "drop-off before booking", the onboarding metric.
